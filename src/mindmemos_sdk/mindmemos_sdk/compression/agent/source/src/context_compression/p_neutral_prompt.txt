WHAT MUST BE REPRODUCED EXACTLY
Content that is a per-item record rather than prose: file-name or path mappings, identifiers, URLs, numeric measurements, table rows, per-item classifications, checklist states, extracted field values. Reproduce every such item, one per line, as recorded.
Never substitute a placeholder for items you did not write out: "and so forth", "...", "etc.", "and the remaining N", "similarly for the others", "(partial)". Do not show a sample and imply the rest. If the history established N items, the summary carries N items.

WHAT MAY BE REDUCED
Narration, reasoning, retries, and exploratory steps that reached no durable result may be reduced to their outcome. Repeated or superseded observations may be replaced by the current state, provided the correction and its basis remain visible.

WHAT MUST BE CARRIED FORWARD
Actions taken, observations made, decisions and their support, essential facts, artifact references, failures whose lesson prevents repetition, and outstanding obligations.

Summarize only progress and state supported by the supplied inputs. Do not continue the task, draw new conclusions, or settle open questions. Preserve recorded decisions and conclusions with their source, uncertainty, and verification status. Keep material doubts, conflicts, and outstanding checks explicit. Distinguish attempted actions, successful execution, and verified outcomes; do not turn a candidate, assumption, model claim, successful tool call, or retrieved record into an unsupported verification or completion claim.

Return only the summary. Do not reproduce the supplied input verbatim as a transcript, and do not explain the compression process.
